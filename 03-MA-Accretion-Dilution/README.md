# 03 — M&A Accretion/Dilution Model (BSX–Penumbra)

## Overview

Accretion/dilution analysis for Boston Scientific's $14.5B acquisition of Penumbra, built entirely from SEC filings. Reconciled GAAP Year-1 dilution of $0.23/share to management's guided $0.06–$0.08 adjusted EPS impact.

## Deal Terms

- Deal Value: $14.5B (73% cash / 27% stock)
- Target Revenue: $1.4B growing at 17% annually
- GAAP Dilution: $0.23/share Year 1
- Adjusted Dilution (mgmt guidance): $0.06–$0.08/share Year 1

## Key Insight

The $0.17/share gap between GAAP and adjusted dilution is driven by amortization of acquired intangibles and deal-related costs — standard adjustments management excludes but GAAP requires.
