# 02 — Boeing Long-Range Plan (FY2026–FY2030)

## Overview

5-year Long-Range Plan model for Boeing with 10 cascading driver inputs. Surfaced $49B cumulative free cash flow burn under base-case turnaround scenario despite a projected 2029 EBIT inflection.

## 10 Driver Inputs

1. 737 Deliveries
2. 787 Deliveries
3. 777X Deliveries (starting 2027)
4. Avg BCA Price per Aircraft
5. BDS Revenue
6. BCA Operating Margin %
7. BDS Operating Margin %
8. SG&A as % of Total Revenue
9. R&D Spend
10. CapEx

## Key Insight

Even under a successful turnaround (EBIT turns positive by FY2029), cumulative free cash flow burn reaches ~$49B — meaning Boeing needs sustained external financing well beyond the EBIT inflection point.
