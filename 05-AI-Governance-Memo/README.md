# 05 — AI Governance Memo for FP&A

## Overview

Internal memo outlining a 6-month roadmap to introduce AI into FP&A responsibly. Built on AICPA/CIMA's January 2026 AI governance framework.

## 5 Governance Controls

1. Human-in-the-Loop validation — every AI output reviewed before use
2. AI Inventory & Touchpoint Mapping — document every AI deployment
3. Data Governance & Access Controls — enterprise tools only, redaction protocols
4. Center of Excellence & Oversight Body — cross-functional team with audit committee reporting
5. Change Management & Controlled Pilots — pilot before broad deployment

## 3-Tier Training Plan (AICPA AI Skills Accelerator)

- **Strategic** — senior leaders on governance and ethics
- **Transitional** — FP&A team on policies and prompt engineering
- **Operational** — hands-on users on productivity workflows

## 4 Success Metrics

- Time savings (4 hrs → 30 min per close)
- Quality (error catch rate during human review)
- Adoption (% team trained, approved use cases)
- Risk (zero data leakage or unverified claims reaching board)
